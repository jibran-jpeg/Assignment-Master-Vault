# ⚖️ 03.3 - Business Technology & Ethics Playbook

## 📌 Frameworks & Core Applications
1. **Duty-Based Ethics (Kantian Deontology):**
   * Categorical Imperative: Universality & Formula of Humanity (never treat persons merely as a means).
2. **Rights-Based Ethics:**
   * Negative vs. Positive rights; informational privacy, bodily autonomy, informed consent, procedural due process.
3. **Social Contract Theory (Rawlsian Ethics):**
   * Implicit workplace covenant; the **Veil of Ignorance** thought experiment.
4. **Utilitarianism (Act vs. Rule):**
   * Aggregating net utility; why short-term profits fail the long-term utility test once trust collapses.
5. **Virtue Ethics (Aristotelian):**
   * Moral character of corporate leaders (phronesis / practical wisdom, honesty, courage vs. cowardice/greed).

---

## 🏛️ Ethics Management Program (EMP) Architecture
Every EMP proposal must detail:
* **The Charter:** Formal Code of Technological Conduct.
* **The Oversight:** Chief Ethics Officer & Corporate Ethics Advisory Board.
* **The Safe Channel:** Encrypted anonymous hotline + strict zero-tolerance anti-retaliation policy.
* **The Education:** Scenario-based workshops for engineers and managers.
* **The Verification:** Independent algorithmic and data privacy audits.
