# ✍️ 03.5 - English, Humanities & Technical Writing Playbook

## 📌 Anti-AI Humanities Rules
* **Ban the 5-Paragraph Robotic Essay:** Do not write an essay with [Intro with thesis sentence] $ightarrow$ [Body 1] $ightarrow$ [Body 2] $ightarrow$ [Body 3] $ightarrow$ [Restated thesis conclusion]. Real humanities students use organic narrative flow.
* **Close Textual Reading:** Directly quote relevant primary sources, dissecting specific word choices, rhetorical devices, and cultural contexts.
* **Dialectical Complexity:** Acknowledge genuine contradictions, counter-arguments, and unresolved historical tensions rather than forcing a clean, overly tidy resolution.
* **Voice:** Passionate, analytical, and critical. Avoid bland, neutral middle-ground summaries.
