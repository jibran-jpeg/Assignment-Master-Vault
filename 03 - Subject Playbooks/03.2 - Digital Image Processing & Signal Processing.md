## 📌 Core Mathematical Foundations
* **Continuous to Discrete 2D Sampling:**
  $$f[m, n] = f(m \Delta x, n \Delta y)$$
* **Photodiode Aperture Integration:**
  $$g[m, n] = \iint_{A_{m, n}} f(x, y) \cdot h(x - m\Delta x, y - n\Delta y) \, dx \, dy$$
* **2D Nyquist-Shannon Sampling Theorem:**
  $$f_{sx} \ge 2 u_{max}, \quad f_{sy} \ge 2 v_{max}$$
* **Quantization Step Size & Levels:**
  $$L = 2^k, \quad q = rac{V_{max} - V_{min}}{2^k}$$
* **Signal-to-Quantization-Noise Ratio (SQNR):**
  $$	ext{SQNR (dB)} pprox 6.02 k + 1.76 	ext{ dB}$$
* **Uncompressed Raster File Size:**
  $$\text{Size (MB)} = \frac{M \times N \times k \times C}{8 \times 1024 \times 1024}$$
* **Continuous Histogram Equalization Transfer Function:**
  $$s = T(r) = (L-1) \int_0^r p_r(w)\,dw = (L-1) F_r(r)$$
* **Discrete Histogram Equalization Mapping:**
  $$s_k = T(r_k) = \text{round}\left[ (L-1) \sum_{j=0}^k p_r(r_j) \right] = \text{round}\left[ \frac{L-1}{MN} \sum_{j=0}^k n_j \right]$$
* **Histogram Specification (Matching) Inversion:**
  $$s = T(r), \quad v = G(z) \implies z = G^{-1}(s) = G^{-1}(T(r))$$
  $$\hat{z}_k = \arg\min_{z_q} |G(z_q) - s_k|$$

## 🚫 Critical Mathematical Typography & Zero-Box Law
* **NO Callout Boxes for Math:** Equations must NEVER be enclosed in 1x1 tables, shaded cells, or left-bordered callout boxes. They must be formatted as borderless centered paragraphs.
* **Math Font:** Use Times New Roman with italic variables ($r$, $s$, $z$, $k$, $f(x, y)$) and native Word subscripts/superscripts. Never use monospace Consolas for mathematical equations.
