# 📐 03.4 - Mathematics, Calculus & Applied Physics Playbook

## 📌 Key Execution Rules
* **Never Skip Arithmetic Steps:** LLMs notoriously jump from problem statements directly to answers. Real students write down every line of algebraic substitution, integral limits, and trigonometric simplification.
* **Explicit Dimensional Analysis:** Always carry physical units through calculations (e.g., $	ext{kg}\cdot	ext{m/s}^2 = 	ext{N}$, $	ext{W/m}^2$, $	ext{J}$).
* **Free-Body Diagrams & Plots:** When forces, vectors, or functions are involved, generate clean matplotlib plots showing vector arrows and labeled axes.
* **Clear State Statements:**
  * Given / Known Variables: List symbols and numerical values with units.
  * Required / Unknown Variables: Clearly stated objective.
  * Governing Formulae: State general physical law before substituting numbers.
  * Step-by-Step Calculation: Intermediate lines of algebra.
  * Final Conclusion: Boxed or bold numerical answer with physical units.
