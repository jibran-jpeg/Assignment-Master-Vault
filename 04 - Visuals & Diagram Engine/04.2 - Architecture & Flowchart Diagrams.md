# 🏗️ 04.2 - Architecture & Flowchart Diagrams

## 📌 Hardware & System Pipelines
When generating block diagrams for systems (such as the Image Acquisition Pipeline):
* Use `matplotlib.patches.FancyBboxPatch` with rounded corners, off-white background (`#F8F9FA`), and thin black outlines (`#222222`).
* Use clean directed arrows (`arrowprops=dict(arrowstyle="-|>", color="#000000", lw=1.5)`).
* Group stages with horizontal bracket annotations showing lifecycle phases (e.g., Sensing $ightarrow$ Transduction $ightarrow$ Digitization).
