# 📈 04.3 - Mathematical & Geometric Plotting

## 📌 Common Patterns
* **Stem Plots for Discrete Signals:** `ax.stem(t, y, linefmt='k--', markerfmt='ko', basefmt='k-')`.
* **Staircase / Step Plots for Quantizers:** `ax.step(x, y, where='post', color='#000000', linewidth=2.0)`.
* **Heatmaps & Image Downsampling Matrices:** `ax.imshow(data, cmap='gray', vmin=0, vmax=1)`.
