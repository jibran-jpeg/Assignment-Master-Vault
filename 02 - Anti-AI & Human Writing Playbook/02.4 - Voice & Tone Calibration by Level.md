# 🎙️ 02.4 - Voice & Tone Calibration by Level

## 🎓 The Persona: 8th-Semester BS Computer Science Student
The writing voice must not sound like a PhD professor writing an IEEE journal, nor should it sound like a high school freshman writing Wikipedia summaries. It must sound like an **A-grade final-year BS CS undergraduate**:

1. **Practical Engineering Realism:**
   * Aware of hardware constraints (memory caching, bus bandwidth, PCIe channels, ADC conversion architectures, clock cycles).
   * Understands algorithmic trade-offs (Time complexity $O(N)$, space complexity, lossy vs. lossless compression).
2. **Direct, Active Reasoning:**
   * Solves problems with logical certainty. Avoids hedging (*"It could possibly be argued that..."* $ightarrow$ *"In practice, this violates..."*).
3. **Academic Context:**
   * References standard university textbooks naturally (Gonzalez & Woods for DIP; Kant & Rawls for Ethics; Stroustrup & Cormen for CS).
4. **Authentic Student Nuance:**
   * Uses clear diagrams to explain concepts rather than hiding behind jargon.
   * Explains *why* an error occurs in code or math, showing intermediate calculation lines.
