## ⚡ Core Rules at a Glance

1. **Cover Page Integrity:** Never redesign Page 1. Match fonts, lines, logos, and outer borders identically.
2. **Standard Black Only:** All answers, labels, and headings must be `#000000` (strict ban on AI cyan/blue/teal accents).
3. **No Em-Dashes:** Never use `—` or `–` in section titles. Use standard hyphens `-` or colons `:`.
4. **No CSS/Notion Callout Boxes:** Absolute ban on single-cell tables with thick left borders and shaded backgrounds for text summaries in Word.
5. **APA 7th Edition Tables Only:** Three horizontal rules only (top, header-bottom, bottom); zero vertical lines; Table N caption above.
6. **No Cookie-Cutter Key-Value Repeats:** Avoid formulaic identical schemas repeated across sections; vary presentation format organically.
7. **No AI Clichés:** Ban *"In conclusion"*, *"Delving into"*, *"Crucial to note"*, *"It is important to remember"*, etc.
8. **Marks Proportionality:** Match answer depth and length strictly to the question marks.
9. **Both DOCX & PDF:** Always output clean `.docx` in the subject folder and convert to `.pdf` via LibreOffice.
